Audible 2 for 1 Sale: How to Get Two Books for One Credit

By Thomas Carman

Updated on Jun 17, 2025 | Update for Audiobooks

There are few things more satisfying than finding a great deal, especially when it comes to expanding your library of audiobooks. For avid listeners, an Audible credit is a valuable currency. The Audible 2 for 1 sale, also known as a BOGO (Buy One, Get One) sale, is one of the best opportunities for members to maximize that value.

If you've ever wondered how these sales work, when to expect them, or how to snag the very best books, you've come to the right place. This guide will walk you through everything you need to know to become a master of the Audible 2 for 1 sale and other promotions.

Part 1. What is the Audible 2 for 1 Sale?

The premise of this popular promotion is simple yet powerful. During the sale, active Audible members can choose two audiobooks from a special, curated collection and purchase both for a single Audible credit.

This deal is an exclusive benefit for Audible Premium Plus subscribers. It effectively doubles the value of your credit, allowing you to grow your personal library at a significantly reduced cost. It's a fantastic way to discover new authors or dive into a series you've had your eye on without burning through all your credits. While the 2 for 1 is a member favorite, Audible also offers other deals, such as the Daily Deal (available to everyone) and periodic cash sales with deep discounts.

Part 2. How to Use Audible Sale 2 for 1

Navigating the sale for the first time can be a little confusing. The process can differ slightly depending on your device, but the key is to find the sale banner and add two eligible titles to your cart. While recent updates have improved in-app access, many users still find the smoothest experience is on the Audible website.

Step 1Find the Sale

When a 2 for 1 sale is active, it's typically advertised with a large banner on the Audible homepage. Clicking this banner will take you directly to the sale page, which houses the entire collection of eligible titles.

Step 2Browse the Selection

Once on the sale page, you can browse all the audiobooks included in the offer. You can filter by genre, new releases, or other categories to narrow down your choices.

Step 3Add Two Books to Your Cart

Click the button to add your first desired audiobook. The site or app will then prompt you to find your second book. Once you've added the second title, both will be in your special BOGO cart.

Step 4Checkout with One Credit

Once you have exactly two books in your sale cart, the checkout button will become active. Click it to proceed. On the final checkout page, you will see the total cost is just one credit for both audiobooks. Confirm the purchase to add them to your library.

Part 3. When is the Next Audible 2 for 1 Sale?

This is the million-dollar question for many Audible members. While there isn't a fixed, public schedule, these sales tend to occur periodically, often about once a month. However, this frequency can change, and sometimes a month might be skipped, particularly around major holidays when other promotions might be running.

The best way to ensure you never miss a sale is to subscribe to Audible's promotional emails. You will receive an email notification as soon as a new "2 for 1" or other special deal begins. It's also a good habit to check the "Sales & Deals Page" page on the website every so often.

Part 4. Troubleshooting Audible BOGO Sale Not Working

If you're running into issues, it's likely due to one of a few common problems. The official Audible help page on BOGO sales notes that having more than two items in your sale cart can block the purchase. The limit is strictly two audiobooks.

Also, double-check that you have an active Premium Plus membership and at least one credit available. Finally, ensure the titles you've selected are part of the official sale collection, as regular titles are not eligible.

Part 5. FAQs About Audible 2 for 1 Sale

Do I need to be an Audible member to access the 2 for 1 sale?

Yes, these sales are an exclusive perk for active Audible Premium Plus members. You must have a current membership and at least one available credit to participate in the offer.

How often do Audible 2 for 1 sales happen?

There is no official, guaranteed schedule. However, based on past promotions, Audible typically holds a major sale—like a 2 for 1 BOGO or a sitewide cash sale—about once a month. The best way to stay informed is to subscribe to Audible's promotional emails.

What happens if I return an audiobook I bought in a 2 for 1 deal?

To receive your credit back, you must return both Audible audiobooks that were part of the 2-for-1 deal. Because the two books were purchased together as a single transaction for one credit, the system treats them as a matched pair. If you only return one of the audiobooks, you will not receive a credit refund. You must process the return for both titles to have the single credit returned to your account.

Can I buy four books for two credits in the same transaction?

No, the 2 for 1 sale is designed for a single transaction of two eligible books for one credit. If you wish to purchase four books, you would need to complete two separate 2 for 1 transactions, assuming you have enough credits.

Is a "BOGO" sale the same as a "2 for 1" sale?

Yes. BOGO stands for "Buy One, Get One", and Audible uses this term and "2 for 1" interchangeably to describe the same promotion where you get two select audiobooks for the price of one credit.
